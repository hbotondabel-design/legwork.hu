Questions

What do you need from me?

A few sentences about your business, some photos and your exact contact details. If you have no good photos, we sort it out together.

What if I like none of the samples?

Then you owe nothing. If you have an idea, I am happy to make a version in a different direction.

Who owns the site and the domain?

Once finished and paid for, the site is yours. The domain can be registered in your name, and if you ever move elsewhere, I hand over every file and login.

How long does it take?

We fix the deadline in the quote, counted from when the texts and photos arrive.

How do you handle my data?

Only to reply to you and, if we work together, for the job. Details are in the privacy notice (in Hungarian).

Do you do bookkeeping?

No. Automation is for internal reports and spreadsheets. It does not replace your accountant.
